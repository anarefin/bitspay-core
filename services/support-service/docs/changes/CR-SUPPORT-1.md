## 1. Motivation
SRS v1.2 (14 Oct 2026) adds the release value `MVP` before R1. The MVP needs back-office reversal (SUP-006) and wrong-transfer handling (SUP-005); the rest of support (other disputes, complaints beyond what a reversal needs, SLA / dashboards, customer 360, CRM SUP-010) is R1+. The MVP also adds bill payment (BIL-011, UC-16) and mobile recharge (BIL-012, UC-17) as transaction types `BILL_PAY` (ledger command 18) and `RECHARGE` (command 19); both are reversible by ledger `post-reversal`, so support must accept them in reversal requests.
