## 1. Motivation
SRS v1.2 (14 Oct 2026) adds the MVP release before R1. Two of its products are new transaction types owned by transaction-service: `BILL_PAY` (BIL-011, internal biller catalogue, simulated settlement) and `RECHARGE` (BIL-012, mobile recharge via the MNO catalogue, simulated fulfilment). transaction-service calls `POST /v1/risk/evaluate` for both, with the customer as the `from` party and the biller settlement wallet (type `B`) as the `to` party. limits-risk must accept the new txnTypes and wallet type, and seed customer limits for them (LIM-002, values provisional under SRS B-26). FRD-003: the rule library gains one recharge-velocity rule in SHADOW.
