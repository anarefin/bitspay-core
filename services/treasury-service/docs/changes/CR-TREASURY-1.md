## 1. Motivation
SRS v1.2 (14 Oct 2026) adds a new release value **MVP** that precedes R1. Its supporting capabilities include "treasury e-money issuance and agent float" (admin switch `FLOAT_TRANSFER` stays enabled). Treasury must therefore deliver EMY-001, EMY-003, EMY-005, EMY-007, EMY-009 (issuance with the hard control and its trust-account / confirmation prerequisite), LIQ-003, LIQ-004 (RETURN to PARENT) and AGT-005 (agent float requests), and BOM-004/005 (maker-checker) in the MVP. All other treasury requirements (EMY-002, EMY-004 snapshot part, EMY-008, LIQ-001/002/005/006, BOM-006, REC-009 inputs) move to R1+; EMY-006 and LIQ-008 stay R2. None of the withdrawn v1.2 IDs (GLD-031, INT-01, B-18) or reworded LED-014 / A-37 are referenced by this pack.
