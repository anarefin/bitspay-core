## 1. Motivation
SRS v1.2 (14 Oct 2026) adds a new release `MVP` before R1. Its out-of-MVP (R1+) list includes finance-ops reconciliation and reporting, so the whole finance-ops-service is not delivered in the MVP; the MVP books are kept by general-ledger-service (GL-R1a). SRS v1.2 also removes every GL journal export notion: `LED-014` is reworded to "Ledger accounts shall be mapped to the Operator's chart of accounts by the General Ledger service (GLD-006, GLD-013); the real-time view is the GL open-day overlay (GLD-030).", and `GLD-031`, `INT-01`, `B-18` are withdrawn. The general ledger keeps the books and produces reports only.
